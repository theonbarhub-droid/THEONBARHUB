**The 2027 Ontario licensing exams** are on these dates:

- **Barrister:** February 25, June 8 and November 2, 2027
- **Solicitor:** March 11, June 22 and November 16, 2027

The exam date is rarely the deadline that catches people. The licensing process application closes months earlier, and you must apply and pay before you can register for a sitting. Below is every deadline, from the Law Society of Ontario.

## Every 2027 sitting and its deadlines

| Sitting | Exam date | Licensing application | Accommodation requests | Register, defer or cancel by | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winter Barrister | Feb 25, 2027 | Nov 10, 2026 (closed) | Jan 12, 2027 | Jan 12, 2027 | 2026-27 |
| Winter Solicitor | Mar 11, 2027 | Nov 10, 2026 (closed) | Jan 12, 2027 | Jan 27, 2027 | 2026-27 |
| Summer Barrister | Jun 8, 2027 | Mar 1, 2027 | Apr 28, 2027 | Apr 28, 2027 | 2027-28 |
| Summer Solicitor | Jun 22, 2027 | Mar 1, 2027 | Apr 28, 2027 | May 12, 2027 | 2027-28 |
| Fall Barrister | Nov 2, 2027 | Jul 28, 2027 | Sep 20, 2027 | Sep 20, 2027 | 2027-28 |
| Fall Solicitor | Nov 16, 2027 | Jul 28, 2027 | Sep 20, 2027 | Oct 4, 2027 | 2027-28 |

The first sittings of 2028 are Barrister on February 22 and Solicitor on March 7, 2028. Our [results date tracker](/tools/results-tracker) shows all of these with live countdowns.

## Four things to know before you pick a sitting

1. **Register by the deadline, or wait for the next sitting.** The registration deadline is also the last day to defer or cancel.
2. **Accommodation requests close early.** For winter 2027 they close on January 12, 2027, the same day as Barrister registration and two weeks before Solicitor registration.
3. **Materials change in April.** The 2027-28 study materials are released on April 13, 2027 and apply from the June sittings. If you study for March, you use the 2026-27 materials. If you move to June, plan for the new edition.
4. **Summer starts a new licensing year.** Moving from a winter 2027 sitting to summer 2027 brings a new application deadline of March 1, 2027.

## Which sitting should you choose?

- **Choose the earliest sitting you can be ready for**, with at least 8 to 10 weeks of focused study for a first attempt at either exam.
- **Space the two exams sensibly.** Each season the Barrister and Solicitor are two weeks apart. Writing both in one season works if you studied for both in advance; if not, consider writing one now and one next season.
- **Retaking after November 2026?** See our [March 11, 2027 retake plan](/blog/failed-solicitor-exam-retake-plan-march-2027).

All dates are from the [Law Society of Ontario's exam dates page](https://lso.ca/becoming-licensed/lawyer-licensing-process/licensing-examinations/examination-dates-and-deadlines), checked November 20, 2026. The LSO says dates are subject to change, so confirm on lso.ca before you register.
