The LSO licensing exams are long, high-pressure days. Being organized about what you bring reduces stress and prevents avoidable problems at check-in. This checklist covers what to bring, what to leave at home, and what to expect.

A critical note before anything else: **the LSO sends specific instructions before each exam** that list exactly what is required and prohibited at your test centre. Those instructions govern. This checklist is a general guide to help you prepare, not a substitute for your official instructions. Read them carefully and follow them.

## The Essentials

**Government-issued photo ID.** You cannot write the exam without valid identification that matches the name on your registration. Bring it. If your ID name differs from your registration (maiden name, legal name change, spelling differences), resolve this with the LSO well before exam day, not at check-in.

**Your exam confirmation.** Bring confirmation of your registration and your assigned test centre and time. Know your exact start time and arrive early.

**Your study materials.** The licensing exams allow candidates to bring their own printed study materials. This is why indexing matters so much. Bring:

- Your printed, bound study materials
- Your index or table of contents
- Your tabs and navigation system

Confirm the current rules on materials in your official instructions. Rules about binding, tabbing, and annotations can be specific.

## Your Materials System

The exams are open-material, but they are also time-pressured. You will not have time to read your materials during the exam. You need to **find** answers fast.

Candidates who pass tend to have:

- A detailed index that maps topics to page numbers
- Clear tabs on major sections
- Materials printed and bound so pages do not fall out or get out of order
- A consistent system they have practised with during mock exams

Do not show up with pristine, unindexed materials. The time to build your navigation system is during study, not on exam day.

## What to Wear

**Layers.** Test centres can be cold or warm, and you have no control over it. Wear layers you can add or remove. Being too cold or too hot for several hours hurts your focus.

**Comfort.** You will sit for hours. Wear something you can concentrate in.

## Food, Water, and Breaks

Rules on food and water vary by test centre. Water in a clear container is often permitted. Check your instructions.

If permitted, bring:

- Water in a clear bottle
- A small snack for any break period

Know the break structure for your exam in advance so you can plan when to eat and drink.

## What to Leave at Home

Prohibited items commonly include:

- Phones and smartwatches (you will have to store these; do not rely on having them during the exam)
- Laptops, tablets, and other electronics
- Loose notes beyond what is permitted
- Bags brought to your seat (storage is usually provided but keep it minimal)

Check your official instructions for the complete prohibited list. Bringing a prohibited item into the exam room can have serious consequences.

## Getting There

**Know your route.** Confirm your test centre location the day before. Plan your travel with extra time for delays.

**Arrive early.** Check-in takes time, especially with hundreds of candidates. Arriving early reduces stress and gives you margin if something goes wrong.

**Parking and transit.** If you are driving, know where you will park. If you are taking transit, check the schedule and have a backup.

## The Night Before

- Lay out your ID, confirmation, and materials.
- Confirm your test centre and start time.
- Reread your official LSO instructions one more time.
- Sleep. A rested brain outperforms a crammed, exhausted one on a multiple-choice exam where speed of recall matters.

## At the Test Centre

Expect:

- Identity verification at check-in
- A check of your materials against the permitted-items rules
- Storage of prohibited items
- Being seated and given instructions before the exam begins

Follow the invigilators' directions. If you have a question, ask before the exam starts.

## The Short Version

Bring: valid photo ID, your confirmation, your indexed and bound materials, water if permitted, layers.

Leave: phone, smartwatch, electronics, anything on the prohibited list.

Do: arrive early, know your route, read your official instructions.

Everything else is noise. Get these basics right and you walk in able to focus on the exam itself.
