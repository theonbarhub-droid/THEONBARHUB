**Short answer:** we expect November 2026 **Barrister** results between **December 10 and 18, 2026**, and **Solicitor** results between **January 6 and 13, 2027**. These are estimates. The Law Society of Ontario does not publish a results date ahead of time.

You can follow both windows, with live countdowns, on our [results date tracker](/tools/results-tracker).

## Our estimate for each exam

| Exam | Written | Estimated results window | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barrister | Tuesday, November 3, 2026 | December 10 to 18, 2026 | Past Barrister results have landed roughly 5 to 7 weeks after the exam. November results have come out before the holiday break. |
| Solicitor | Tuesday, November 17, 2026 | January 6 to 13, 2027 | Past Solicitor results have taken roughly 6 to 8 weeks. For November sittings the holidays push release into the first or second week of January. |

## How we built the estimate

The LSO gives no date, so we looked at when results actually appeared for past sittings, as reported by candidates at the time. This is the pattern:

| Sitting | Results appeared |
| --- | --- |
| Barrister, November 2024 | Friday, December 13, 2024 |
| Solicitor, November 2024 | Thursday, January 9, 2025 |
| Barrister, winter 2025 | Thursday, March 20, 2025 |
| Solicitor, winter 2025 | Wednesday, April 9, 2025 |
| Barrister, summer 2025 | Monday, July 14, 2025 |
| Solicitor, summer 2025 | Wednesday, July 30, 2025 |
| Solicitor, November 2025 | Wednesday, January 7, 2026 |
| Solicitor, winter 2026 | Wednesday, April 8, 2026 |
| Barrister, summer 2026 | Friday, July 17, 2026 |

Three things stand out:

1. **No fixed weekday.** Results have come on every weekday. Recent Solicitor releases have often fallen on a Wednesday, but that is a tendency, not a rule.
2. **Barrister comes back faster than Solicitor.** The Barrister exam is usually marked a week or two sooner after the sitting.
3. **The holidays matter.** For November Solicitor sittings, every recent release has come in the first half of January.

> These dates come from candidates reporting their results as they arrived. Treat the windows as a guide for planning your weeks, not a promise.

## How results show up

Results appear in your LSO Connects account. Candidates commonly report that the status on their exam changes shortly before the results themselves become visible, and that the portal can be slow when everyone logs in at once. If the page will not load on results day, wait and try again rather than refreshing every few seconds.

When results are out, the question "when will results be released?" stops being the most asked question about the exam. Of the 2,500 public candidate posts we analysed, more than 90 asked exactly that. You are not the only one refreshing.

## What to do while you wait

- **Do not stop studying completely if you have the other exam ahead.** If you wrote the Barrister in November and the Solicitor is still to come, keep going.
- **Know your next deadline now.** If you might need to rewrite, the winter Solicitor exam is **March 11, 2027** and the registration deadline is **January 27, 2027**. The accommodation request deadline for the winter sittings is **January 12, 2027**, which can fall before Solicitor results arrive.
- **Keep your materials.** The winter 2027 sittings use the same 2026-27 study materials as November. Summer 2027 sittings switch to new 2027-28 materials, released April 13, 2027.
- **Plan something unrelated for the week the window opens.** You will check LSO Connects anyway; give yourself something else to do between checks.

If the news is not what you hoped for, read our [March 11, 2027 retake plan](/blog/failed-solicitor-exam-retake-plan-march-2027). It is built for the eight weeks between January results and the winter exam.
